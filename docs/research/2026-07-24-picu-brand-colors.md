# Research: color identities of the (pediatric) critical-care world

Date: 2026-07-24 · Method: 7-agent workflow reading the live official sites
(computed CSS, brand assets, pixel-sampled logos — hex reported only where
observed; approximations labeled). Full raw findings in the session
workflow output; this file is the decision-grade synthesis.

## The overuse map

- **Blue is the field's universal anchor.** SCCM royal blue `#0055A5`,
  ESICM ultramarine `#000EBC`, WFICC marine navy, WFPICCS blue `#005596`,
  ESPNIC (an entirely blue system), AAP navy `#00247F`, Boston Children's
  `#003087`, GOSH `#0072CE`, SickKids `#0067B1`. 100% of audited
  critical-care and pediatric institutions anchor on blue.
- **Orange is the overused accent** riding with it: SCCM `#F96403`, ESICM
  `#FF8900`, WFICC `#FF6927`. Blue+orange is the genre's tired pairing.
- **Green reads "official Saudi government health":** the DGA unified
  stylesheet standardizes `#1B8354` across state digital touchpoints; MOH
  is emphatically green+sand. Green is also doubly claimed in child-health
  awareness (NICU awareness; children's mental health).
- **Gold is untouchable** — the international childhood-cancer color.
- **Purple** belongs to prematurity (World Prematurity Day, "Light It Up
  Purple").
- **PICU has no awareness color.** WFPICCS's World PICU Awareness Week
  specifies none — the space is unclaimed.

## The distinctive moves by kin organizations

- **Saudi Critical Care Society: RED.** Red SCCS wordmark + dark-green
  emblem; brick-red `#C94239` web theme. The society closest to
  TowardPCC's home made the boldest non-blue choice in the field.
  (Verified via Internet Archive June 2024 capture — the live domain was
  unreachable and the old domain is squatted; noted for caution.)
- **PCICS (pediatric cardiac ICU): deep cardiac red `#AE1E22`.**
- **RCPCH (royal pediatric college): near-black indigo + magenta
  `#E00087`** — magenta as the site-wide link color; instantly
  recognizable among medical colleges.
- **Saudi Pediatric Association:** playful multicolor logo; assigns
  **magenta `#C83792` to its "clinical tools" category**.
- **PALISI:** slate `#254151` + terracotta `#D55342` + gold `#F4CE50` —
  a softer editorial register.
- **MOH's secondary sand-gold `#958A5F`** — flagged by the research as a
  distinctive, less-copied companion color in the Saudi context.

## Evidence literature (used as preference signals only — no overclaims)

- Tofle et al. 2004 (Center for Health Design): no proven color→outcome
  links; associations are cultural/learned; warns against universal rules.
- Park & Park 2013 (HERD): pediatric patients prefer blue/green rooms;
  stark white least preferred; preferences vary by culture.
- Cai et al. 2021: warm colors preferred in family-facing waiting areas.
- Tabanejad 2024 systematic review: evidence remains limited; cool =
  calming, children like light cheerful hues.

## Implications for TowardPCC

1. Avoid blue and teal anchors entirely (founder directive + saturation).
2. Avoid green anchor (reads Saudi-gov; awareness-claimed), gold anchor
   (childhood cancer), purple anchor (prematurity).
3. The legitimate, kin-grounded non-blue families are **crimson/red**
   (SCCS, PCICS, CHD) and **magenta** (RCPCH, SPA clinical tools), plus a
   **warm desert register** (MOH sand-gold companion; family-facing warmth
   evidence) usable as support rather than anchor.
4. Red discipline if chosen: brand crimson must never double as the error
   color — alerts/errors get their own distinct treatment (amber + icon),
   and red appears as accent, never as large alarm fields.
5. PICU's unclaimed color space means TowardPCC can genuinely own
   whatever it picks.
